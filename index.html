FAQ

Common questions

Can I buy just one app?

Yes. Every app is sold on its own with included storage. If you buy a second app, both share one pool and the storage adds up.

What happens to my Capsyl account?

Your Capsyl content moves over as is. Photos and Memories go to Photos, File Manager and Documents to Drive, Locked Folder to Vault, and Genius edits to Genius. Your existing storage carries into the matching plan.

How does shared storage work across apps?

Your plan sets one total. Each app shows what it uses, and the pool view in your account shows the split. When you approach the limit you can upgrade or clear space from any app.

Do JellyToast guests need an account?

No. Guests join an event from a link and upload photos and videos without signing up. Uploads count against the host's pool.

Which AI features are included?

AI search is in every plan. Premium and Family include unlimited Genius edits, document summaries, audio clean-up and event reels. Essentials includes 100 AI credits a month.
